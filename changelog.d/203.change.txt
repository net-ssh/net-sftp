`upload!` and `download!` now give new files and directories the source's permissions (masked to 0777, subject to the umask) like OpenSSH's sftp client, so executables stay executable and private files are no longer created world-readable. The new `:preserve` option also copies access and modification times and sets permissions exactly. As with OpenSSH, attributes are best effort: if they can't be read the transfer uses default permissions, and if they can't be set a warning is logged and the transfer still succeeds.
